Republic Act No. 6713: Code of Conduct & Ethical Standards

ReviewTayo Editorial Team · Curriculum ResearchersSources: Official Gazette · CSC
1. The 8 Norms of Conduct (Section 4)

Every public official and employee must uphold eight norms: commitment to public interest, professionalism, justness and sincerity, political neutrality, responsiveness to the public, nationalism and patriotism, commitment to democracy, and simple living.

Key takeaways for examinees
  • Public resources are never used for personal enrichment.
  • Political neutrality prohibits partisan activity and political discrimination.
  • Simple living: lifestyle must match legitimate government income.
2. SALN deadlines (Section 8)

File a sworn SALN within 30 days after assumption of office, on or before April 30 every year, and within 30 days after separation from service.
